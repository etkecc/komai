# ✨ Message Effects

Some messages can make the timeline feel a little more alive.

Komai recognizes a small set of emojis and special effect messages and can
turn them into playful timeline effects like confetti, sunshine, hearts, rain,
or lightning.

Think of them as tiny bits of atmosphere, not essential message content.

## 🌤️ What They Look Like

Depending on the message, Komai may show effects such as:

- `🎉` or `🎊` for confetti
- `☀️` or `🌞` for sunlight
- `❤️`, `🫶`, `💕`, `😍`, or `🥰` for a soft heart-themed effect
- `🌧️`, `🌦️`, or `☔` for rain
- `⚡` for lightning
- `⛈️` for a short storm with rain and lightning together

These effects stay local to your view of the timeline. They are there to add a
bit of mood, not to change the meaning of the message.

## 💬 Where They Come From

Most effects are triggered by the message itself, usually by emoji.

That means you do not need to learn a long list of slash commands just to see
them. If someone sends a sunny message with `☀️`, Komai can show sunlight. If a
message arrives with `⛈️`, Komai can show a brief storm. If someone fills a
message with hearts like `❤️` or `💕`, Komai can answer with a matching love
effect.

Some older explicit effect messages still exist for compatibility, but the
main idea in Komai is simple:

- expressive message
- expressive timeline

## 🎛️ Turn Them On or Off

You can control this in:

- **Application Settings → Timeline → Show message effects**

If you prefer a quieter timeline, just switch them off.

## 🌈 Why Komai Has Them

Komai is a usability-focused desktop client, but that does not mean every part
of the app has to feel flat.

Message effects are meant to be:

- brief
- readable
- recognizable
- fun without taking over the interface

The goal is a little delight, not chaos.
